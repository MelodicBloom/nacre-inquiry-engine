## Repository-specific records

### D05 — AURELITH NACRE: the multimodal translation substrate

**Use when:** When designing the shared ontology, material genome, visual constructs, transformation rules, or cross-medium compiler.

[Recovered intent] The operating documentation stack describes a provenance-aware translation compiler linking meaning, material, mathematics, pattern, light, space, time, interaction, narrative, culture, implementation, and evidence. Its common record classes are Entity, Parameter, Relationship, and Transformation, supported by assertions, constraints, variants, and decisions.

[Identity preservation] A transformation declares preconditions, adaptable properties, invariants, prohibited loss, loss budgets, parameter bindings, reversibility, fallback, and validation. A variant has a family/genome, seed, permissions, optical/material/temporal profiles, narrative register, and provenance.

[Observed boundary] The stack is Draft 0.1, including a first-slice runbook and explicit exclusions. It does not establish a deployed universal ontology. Its ratification vocabulary and ownership must be reconciled with current canonical repositories before integration.

[Repair resolution — proposed] Start with domain-specific records and a minimal shared envelope. Avoid a universal schema that flattens scientific, cultural, mathematical, and artistic authority. Formal similarity must not imply historical derivation or cultural association. Preserve contradictions as assertions with scope.

[Reimagined horizon] A queryable design-memory system that explains why a choice fits, what it may become, what must survive translation, what changes across scale, and what evidence is still missing. It can support callable design objects and Kinexus without becoming a second hidden source of truth.

[Carry-forward test] Trace one reference/intent through a visual construct, parameter, material recipe, component/shader binding, accessible alternative, and evidence receipt. Validate links and loss declarations; keep cultural/semantic interpretation inspectable and revisable.

3. Map tokens and controls to actual consumers with explicit units, ownership, accessible alternatives, and observable effect.
4. Complete the relevant interaction/error/cancellation states before promoting a reusable component.
5. Pair source/runtime checks with human correspondence review against the actual visual inspiration.
6. Keep proposed repairs unexecuted until a later implementation records its own revision, checks, and evidence.
